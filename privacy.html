How this website is intended to handle information. Review and adapt this draft to match the services and tools actually used before publication.

1. Overview

NEXOVENT Tech aims to respect your privacy. This draft policy describes general practices for information that may be provided when you contact us or browse this website. It must be reviewed against the final website configuration and applicable law.

2. Information you provide

If you contact us by email or through a configured form, we may receive information such as your name, email address, organisation and project details. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary.

3. Website and technical data

Hosting providers, security tools or analytics services may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information and pages requested, depending on the services enabled. This website draft does not itself include an analytics integration.

4. How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, discuss project requirements, maintain website security, and meet legal obligations. We do not claim to sell personal information.

5. Third-party services

Links, externally hosted images, fonts, hosting and form providers may be subject to their own privacy terms. Review the providers used on the live site and disclose them where required.

6. Retention and security

Information should be retained only as long as reasonably needed for its purpose or legal obligations, and protected using appropriate safeguards. No online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion of personal information. Contact contact@nexovent.tech to make a request.

8. Children

This website is intended for business audiences and is not designed to solicit personal information from children.

9. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the website or applicable requirements change. Contact contact@nexovent.tech with privacy questions.
